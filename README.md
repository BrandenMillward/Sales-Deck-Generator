# Sales-Deck-Generator
A AI based workflow and agent used to generate sales decks based on your company and the prospective clients' information, utilising a variety of AI tools


Here is the link to the live demo and presentations https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1t-piWynvE6kobLYCSJOq0iHbXkseJWqe?usp=sharing

## Setup

Before importing the workflow into Zapier, replace `[Your company]`, `https://example.com` and the brand colour placeholders in the prompts with your own company details.
